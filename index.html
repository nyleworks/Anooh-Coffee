    Our Story

    Anooh. Let's go for coffee.

    Anooh Coffee is not an ordinary roadside cafe, it is part of a homegrown journey rooted deeply in Sabah’s highland soil.

    Our Story

    Our story

    Anooh Coffee was founded by Azizul Julirin, a Sabahan agro-entrepreneur from Kundasang. Long before the coffee shop came farming in the highlands: Kundasang Aquafarm, an aquaponics and hydroponics farm Azizul set up in 2015, along with Midori Homestay in Mesilou.

    When COVID-19 and the lockdowns made those years hard for farms and homestays alike, the business looked for a new way forward and turned to food and drink. In late 2022, Anooh Coffee opened its doors.

    Anooh means “jom”, “mari”, or “let’s go” in Dusun.

    It’s an invitation: bring your friends and family, sit down, and have a kopi-kopi together.

    What you’ll find here

    • Freshly ground coffee, from local Sabah beans and imported varieties.
    • Local products from Sabahan small businesses (IKS), so the cottage producers around us grow with us.
    • Thai-style food from our friends at Tuktuk Tomyam, to go with your drink.

    We sit on the main East–West highway, about 3 km from Kundasang town. Anooh grew out of adapting after the pandemic, working hand in hand with our community and taking the business online, and that spirit still shapes how we run things today.
